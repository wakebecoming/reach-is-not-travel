# Privacy Policy — "What Woke Here" uploader

This app exists for one job: uploading videos to its operator's own YouTube channel.

**What it collects:** Your YouTube channel information and the OAuth tokens required to upload videos on your behalf.

**Where it goes:** The tokens live on the operator's own machine. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

**What it doesn't do:** No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.
